Project Overview

Project Title: BlogSense AI : An Agentic AI-Powered Intelligent Writing Assistant

Domain: Media & Content Creation

BlogSense AI combines IBM Granite Models, IBM watsonx.ai, IBM Orchestrate and Langflow to automate research, summarize content, analyze trends, generate SEO-friendly blogs and recommend the best publishing strategy for bloggers.

Problem Statement

The Challenge

Bloggers today face an overwhelming flood of scattered information from news updates, research articles, social media trends, and audience feedback. This information is fragmented, quickly outdated, and difficult to organize.

Instead of focusing on writing quality content, bloggers spend significant time searching, filtering, verifying, and organizing information from multiple platforms.

The challenge is not just collecting ideas but intelligently synthesizing, prioritizing, and transforming information into impactful blogs.

Project Objective

  • Collect real-time information from news websites, research papers, social media platforms and audience feedback.
  • Generate AI-powered summaries and actionable blog topic suggestions.
  • Support multimodal inputs including text, images and voice notes.
  • Recommend blog titles, outlines, keywords and writing tone.
  • Predict trending topics using audience engagement analysis.
  • Suggest hashtags, SEO keywords and best publishing time.
  • Display all insights through a real-time dashboard.

Proposed Solution

BlogSense AI is an Agentic AI-powered intelligent writing assistant developed using IBM Granite Models, IBM watsonx.ai, IBM Orchestrate and Langflow.

The platform automatically gathers information from multiple trusted sources including research papers, news websites, social media platforms and audience comments.

It summarizes content, removes duplicate information, identifies trending topics and generates unique blog ideas for writers.

Users can also upload PDFs, images, text prompts or voice notes. The AI converts these inputs into structured blog outlines, titles, summaries, SEO keywords and complete blog drafts.

A predictive analytics module analyzes likes, comments, shares and audience engagement to recommend:

  • Best publishing time
  • Trending hashtags
  • SEO keywords
  • Target audience
  • Content angle for maximum engagement

The application also provides an interactive dashboard displaying trending blog topics, sentiment analysis, engagement forecasts, source credibility and AI-generated summaries.

Technology Used

Langflow Platform

Designs and orchestrates the complete AI workflow visually. It connects all AI components together and automates the blogging process.

IBM Granite Models

Acts as the core AI engine to generate blog titles, summaries, complete blog posts, SEO keywords, hashtags and writing tone suggestions.

IBM watsonx.ai

Runs IBM Granite Models for blog generation, summarization, question answering and intelligent content refinement.

IBM Orchestrate

Coordinates multiple AI agents such as Research Agent, Blog Agent, Trend Agent and Dashboard Agent for intelligent automation.

Langflow Components Used

Chat Input

Accepts the user's blog topic, keywords and content requests. It acts as the starting point of the AI workflow.

IBM watsonx AI Agent

Acts as the intelligent decision-making component that understands user requirements and coordinates the entire blog writing workflow.

IBM Granite Model

Analyzes collected information and generates blog titles, summaries, detailed blogs, SEO keywords, hashtags and high-quality content.

Chat Output

Displays the final AI-generated blog along with recommendations, trending topics, sentiment analysis and publishing suggestions.

File Component

Allows users to upload PDFs, research papers, articles and blog drafts. The AI extracts useful information and generates relevant blog content.

Langflow Workflow

The workflow begins with user input through Chat Input. The IBM watsonx AI Agent processes the request and coordinates different AI agents. IBM Granite Models generate summaries, blog titles, complete articles, SEO keywords and hashtags. Finally, the Chat Output displays the generated blog along with trend analysis, engagement prediction and publishing recommendations.
